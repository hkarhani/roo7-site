About this analytics view

  • Metrics use observed net account equity. Deposits and withdrawals remain included until a verified cash-flow ledger is available.
  • Benchmark options match the public view (composite basket or individual majors) but the data here is private to your account.
  • Data updates after fresh observations; summary dates use your browser time zone.
  • Missing observations remain gaps. Unverified balances appear only in the recorded-equity graph; market movements are never used to guess cash flows.

* Latest valid observed segment only; hover for its dates. A blank full-period result means coverage is incomplete. Each percentage-chart segment restarts at 0% after a gap.
